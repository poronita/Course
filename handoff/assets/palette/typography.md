# Typography

All three fonts are free for commercial use under the SIL Open Font License. **Bundle the font files in the app** (do not load them from Google at run time, so the privacy promise holds and the app works offline).

| Role | Font | Weights | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Bricolage Grotesque | 600, 800 | Headings, big numbers, level text. Letter spacing -0.01 em. |
| Text | Hanken Grotesk | 400, 600, 700, 800 | Body text, buttons, chips. |
| Numbers and file facts | JetBrains Mono | 400, 600 | File sizes, percentages, URLs, codes. |

Sizes used in the approved mock-up (app layout): screen title 24 px (website 30 px), card title 14.5 to 17 px, body 13 to 14 px, small label 11 to 12.5 px, big counter 38 px (website 52 to 64 px), level text 54 px. Buttons are 46 px high with 15 px text. Tap targets are at least 44 px.
